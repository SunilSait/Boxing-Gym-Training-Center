Head Coach & Founder

Meet the championship mentor and visionary leading the IronJaw coaching team.

Master Coach

Marcus Johnson

Marcus built IronJaw from a small garage gym into the city's premier boxing training center. A former WBC middleweight contender with a record of 28-5-2, Marcus brings real-world fight experience to every coaching session.

His philosophy is simple: boxing is the greatest teacher of discipline, respect, and self-belief. Under his guidance, IronJaw fighters have won 48 championship titles across amateur and professional divisions.

Philosophy

Our Coaching Philosophy

Every session at IronJaw is guided by these core principles.

Respect First

Every member is treated with respect regardless of skill level. The gym is a place of mutual support and encouragement.

Progressive Development

Structured skill progression ensures you're always challenged but never overwhelmed. Growth is measured and intentional.

Mental Toughness

Boxing builds more than muscles — it forges mental resilience, discipline, and an unshakeable confidence.
