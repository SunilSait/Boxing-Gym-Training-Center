Our History

The IronJaw Story

From a small garage gym to the city's premier boxing training center.

2012 — The Beginning

A Garage, a Dream, and Two Heavy Bags

Coach Marcus Johnson opened IronJaw in a converted warehouse with nothing but passion and a vision to make real boxing training accessible to everyone.

2015 — First Champions

Regional Title Wins

Three IronJaw fighters won regional titles in their weight classes, putting the gym on the competitive boxing map and attracting serious talent.

2018 — Expansion

New Facility & Programs

Moved to our current 10,000 sq ft facility. Launched youth programs, women's boxing, and fitness boxing to serve the broader community.

2022 — Digital Era

Member Dashboard & Online Booking

Launched our member portal for class booking, progress tracking, and seamless membership management — training meets technology.

2024 — Today

2,500+ Members Strong

IronJaw stands as the city's #1 boxing gym with 48 championship titles, 15 coaches, and a thriving community of fighters and fitness enthusiasts.
